👩🏻‍💻About me

I work on bridging audio and large language models, from discrete audio representations to benchmarks and interpretability.

I'm finishing my PhD at Mila and Concordia University with Mirco Ravanelli and Cem Subakan. Most recently I worked on generative, expressive speech models at Epic Games. I'm a core contributor to SpeechBrain and founded the Conversational AI Reading Group. 🤝 Always happy to collaborate.
